Roronoa Zoro

Roronoa Zoro is a fictional character from the manga and anime series One Piece.

story

Zoro is a swordsman who dreams of becoming the world's greatest swordsman. He is a member of the Straw Hat Pirates and serves as the crew's combatant. Zoro is known for his incredible strength, endurance, and swordsmanship skills, often wielding three swords in battle known as the "sentoryu".

why him

Zoro is a man of his words. He will die to keep his promises.

His promise with his childhood friend is what drives him towards his dream.
